ID: copy-dash-connector
DOMAIN: copy
NAME: Em dash or spaced hyphen as clause connector
SEVERITY: 3
SIGNATURE: copy|punctuation|em-dash-or-spaced-hyphen+joins-clauses
SIGNALS:
  - Em dash (U+2014) between clauses, with or without spaces around it
  - Spaced hyphen or en dash between words used as a pause: word - word
  - Double hyphen between words standing in for an em dash
  - Several such connectors in one paragraph
STATIC_RULES: copy.dash-connector
WHY:
  Model-written prose leans on the dash to bolt clauses together instead of choosing a real
  sentence structure. The density of dashes is one of the most recognized tells.
ASCII_SLOP:
+--SLOP--
  Our tool is fast [em dash] really fast [em dash] and it just works.
  Setup takes minutes - not hours - and you are done.
+--END--
FIX:
  Split the sentence at the dash or restructure it: use a period, a comma with a
  conjunction, or a colon when a list or explanation follows.
ASCII_FIXED:
+--FIXED--
  Builds finish in 40 seconds on a laptop.
  Setup takes about ten minutes. After that, every push deploys.
+--END--
