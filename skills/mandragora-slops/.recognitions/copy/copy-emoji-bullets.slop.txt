SIGNATURE: copy|list|emoji-as-bullet-marker+repeated
SIGNALS:
  - Two or more list items start with an emoji instead of a word or plain bullet
  - Typical glyphs: check mark, rocket, sparkles, lightning, lock, chart
  - The emoji repeats the meaning of the first word of the item
  - Used in READMEs, release notes and landing page feature lists
STATIC_RULES: copy.emoji-bullets
WHY:
  Emoji bullets are the default list style of model-written READMEs and announcements. They
  decorate without adding meaning and break screen reader flow.

  [sparkles] Beautiful UI
  [chart] Powerful analytics
+--END--
FIX:
  Replace the emoji with plain bullets or numbers and rewrite each item as a specific fact.
  Group related items under one short heading instead of decorating each line.
