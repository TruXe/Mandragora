ID: copy-empty-superlatives
DOMAIN: copy
NAME: Empty superlatives with no measurable claim
SEVERITY: 3
SIGNATURE: copy|claims|superlative-adjective+no-number-or-comparison
SIGNALS:
  - Words such as best-in-class, world-class, industry-leading, unparalleled or cutting-edge
  - Phrases like lightning-fast, blazing fast or state-of-the-art
  - The sentence contains no number, benchmark or named comparison
  - Several superlatives in one paragraph
STATIC_RULES: copy.empty-superlative
WHY:
  A superlative without evidence reads as filler because anyone can claim it. Models produce
  them by default when asked to sound persuasive.
ASCII_SLOP:
+--SLOP--
  Our industry-leading platform delivers lightning-fast,
  best-in-class performance with cutting-edge technology.
+--END--
FIX:
  Replace each superlative with the measurement or comparison it implies, or delete it. Name
  the benchmark, the competitor or the customer result.
ASCII_FIXED:
+--FIXED--
  p95 query time is 48 ms on 2 TB of logs,
  measured on the public ClickBench dataset.
+--END--
SOURCE: seed
