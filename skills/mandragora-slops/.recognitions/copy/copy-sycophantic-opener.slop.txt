ID: copy-sycophantic-opener
DOMAIN: copy
NAME: Sycophantic reply opener
SEVERITY: 3
SIGNATURE: copy|opener|great-question-or-absolutely+praise-before-answer
SIGNALS:
  - Text opens with Great question!, Absolutely! or Certainly!
  - Or with I'd be happy to help before any content
  - The opener praises or agrees before stating anything
  - Common in support replies, docs FAQ answers and chat transcripts
STATIC_RULES: copy.sycophantic-opener
WHY:
  Assistant-style openers are trained politeness that leaks into published text. Readers
  recognize them as a chatbot reply pasted into a page.
ASCII_SLOP:
+--SLOP--
  Great question! Absolutely, you can export your data.
  I'd be happy to help you with that.
+--END--
FIX:
  Delete the opener and start with the answer, then add the steps or the limit that applies.
ASCII_FIXED:
+--FIXED--
  Yes. Settings > Data > Export creates a ZIP of all projects
  (CSV per table). Large workspaces take up to 10 minutes.
+--END--
