ID: model3d-flipped-normals
DOMAIN: model3d
NAME: Flipped or inconsistent normals
SEVERITY: 4
SIGNATURE: model3d|topology|inconsistent-winding+inward-normals
SIGNALS:
  - Adjacent faces traverse their shared edge in the same direction (inconsistent winding)
  - A closed mesh has negative signed volume, so every normal points inward
  - Faces render black or vanish with backface culling enabled
  - Lighting flips from one face to its neighbor on a flat surface
STATIC_RULES: model3d.flipped-normals
WHY:
  Generated meshes stitch patches without a consistent orientation. The result shades in
  stripes and disappears in engines that cull back faces.
ASCII_SLOP:

  +--------+
  |   ^    |   top face points out
  +--------+
  |   v    |   front face points in (flipped)
  +--------+
+--END--
FIX:
  Recalculate normals outside with a consistent winding from one seed face, flip the
  minority faces, then verify signed volume is positive and backface culling shows no holes.
