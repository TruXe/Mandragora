SIGNATURE: texture|composition|mirror-axis+flipped-detail-correlation
SIGNALS:
  - Correlation between the image and its left/right or top/bottom flip of 0.9 or more
  - A visible center line where every feature meets its own reflection
  - Cracks, knots or stains appear in symmetric pairs
  - Often produced to hide a seam by mirroring instead of repainting
STATIC_RULES: texture.mirror-axis
WHY:
  Natural surfaces almost never mirror, so paired features expose the edit at once. Mirroring
  is a common shortcut in generated and quickly tiled textures.
