ID: web-glassmorphism-card
DOMAIN: web
NAME: Glassmorphism card stack
SEVERITY: 3
SIGNATURE: web|card|backdrop-blur+translucent-white-fill+thin-white-border
SIGNALS:
  - backdrop-blur (or CSS backdrop-filter: blur()) on a card or panel
  - Fill is white at 5 to 40 percent opacity, such as bg-white/10
  - A one pixel border in translucent white, such as border-white/20
  - Panel floats over a gradient or blurred color blobs to make the blur visible
  - Several such panels stacked or overlapped at slight offsets
STATIC_RULES: web.glassmorphism
WHY:
  Frosted glass needs a busy background to read at all, so generated pages add gradient blobs
  just to justify it. The translucent fill also drops text contrast below readable levels.

FIX:
  Remove the blur and the translucent layer and place the content on an opaque surface with
  full text contrast. Separate panels by spacing or a single solid divider, and drop the
  decorative blobs that only existed to show the blur.
