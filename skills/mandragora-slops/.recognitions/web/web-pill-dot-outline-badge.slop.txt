SIGNATURE: web|status-badge|pill-shape+inner-dot+outline-matches-dot
SIGNALS:
  - Container is a full pill: rounded-full, or border-radius of 50px or more
  - A 6 to 10 px colored circle sits before the label inside the pill
  - The pill border or ring uses the same hue family as the dot
  - Background is a pale tint of that hue and the text a dark shade of it
  - Label is a one word state such as Active, Live, New, Beta or Online
STATIC_RULES: web.pill-dot-outline-badge
WHY:
  Shape, dot, outline and tint all encode the same state, which a one word label never needs.
  It is the default badge of most component generators and appears unchanged across
  thousands of generated dashboards.
