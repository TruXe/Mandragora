ID: web-rounded-shadow-everywhere
DOMAIN: web
NAME: rounded-2xl and shadow-xl on every container
SEVERITY: 3
SIGNATURE: web|containers|rounded-2xl-or-more+shadow-xl-or-more+applied-everywhere
SIGNALS:
  - Half or more of all styled containers carry rounded-2xl or a larger radius
  - The same containers also carry shadow-xl or shadow-2xl (blur of 20px or more)
  - Cards, inputs wrappers, modals and section wrappers share the same treatment
  - No container uses a flat border or no surface at all for contrast
STATIC_RULES: web.rounded-shadow-ratio
WHY:
  When every box floats with the same large radius and soft shadow, elevation stops meaning
  anything. It is the house style of generated component libraries.
